## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: recruiters and hiring managers deciding whether to interview Dhia Eddine Naija for a full-stack role. They arrive from a link (the site, LinkedIn, GitHub, or email) and need enough proof to start that conversation.

Secondary: people who might hire him for a freelance web project. That path is welcome. A job interview is the main outcome.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that shows Dhia Eddine Naija’s shipped work, experience, certifications, and education, then makes it easy to reach him.

Success is contact after they have seen the real work. That contact can be about a role or a project. Seeing the work without reaching him is not the goal.

## Positioning

He is a full-stack developer in Sousse who points to live sites and their source, not to mock case studies. A neighboring portfolio can copy a layout. It cannot truthfully claim these projects, this name, or these credentials.

## Operating Context

One public site. A visitor scrolls a single page: profile, stack, works, experience, certifications, education, then contact. The contact path is email and the published LinkedIn and GitHub profiles.

The site is the evaluation surface. It is not a product app, a client portal, or a company site.
